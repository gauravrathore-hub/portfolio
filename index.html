Process

What working with me looks like.

No surprises. You get a clear brief, a draft on the agreed date, one round of edits and a page ready to publish, every time.

1. Brief and keyword research

One focus keyword, the related searches and their volumes, the URL slug and the category, before a word is written.

2. Outline

H2s and H3s that mirror what people ask, with a contents box and FAQs where the query needs them.

3. Draft

Long-form, plain English, sourced. Screenshots and steps for how-tos; data and charts for explainers.

4. On-page SEO

Title tag, meta description, alt text, internal links and the keyword placed where it reads naturally.

5. Editor review

Every draft carries a reviewer's name. I have sat on every side of that line: writer, editor and now team lead.

6. Publish and refresh

Formatted in WordPress, then updated as the subject moves. The TikTok guide was refreshed in Feb 2026.
